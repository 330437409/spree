## Summary

The mini program calls 21 gift-card endpoints: buy a card as a product, activate it, receive it, give it to someone else, watch its balance move, and claim subsidised or campaign cards. Spree already models the instrument — `Spree::GiftCard` carries a code, an amount, an amount used, an amount authorised, a status, an expiry and an optional owning customer (`spree/core/app/models/spree/gift_card.rb:25-46`), with apply, remove, redeem and cancel workflows and read-only Store API access at `carts/:cart_id/gift_cards` and `customers/me/gift_cards` (`spree/api/config/routes.rb:56,120`). What is missing is everything around it: buying one, seeing its history, and moving it between people. This plan supplies those by extending the official phase 2 design rather than by building a parallel prepaid instrument — the loyalty plan explicitly forbids a second non-physical flag.

## Key Decisions (do not deviate without discussion)

- **Buying a card is the official phase 2 design, not a new one.** `6.0-6.1-loyalty-gift-cards-store-credits.md:88-106` already specifies it: a `spree_products.gift_card` boolean, variants as denominations, a `spree_gift_card_recipients` row per line item, and idempotent issuance from `order.paid` through `Spree::GiftCards::IssueFromOrder`. `:131` forbids a second "digital-only" flag that would have to be reconciled with `gift_card`. This plan implements that design and adds to it; it does not restate it.
- **A donation is a post-issue transfer and must not become "recipients version two".** A recipient is collected *before* issue, on a cart line, and is part of how the card is born. A donation is a card that already exists and changes hands. They are different tables because they are different moments, and the loyalty plan already rejected putting recipient fields on `spree_line_items` (`:26`).
- **The card gets a ledger, because the mini program shows one.** `Spree::StoreCredit` has `Spree::StoreCreditEvent` (`spree/core/app/models/spree/store_credit_event.rb:9-19`) and a card has no equivalent, yet `giftCardBalanceDetailsPage` is a page of exactly that history. The ledger records issue, redeem, refund, transfer in, transfer out and expiry, with an amount and an actor.
- **A card is a bearer instrument; restricting it to a customer is an action, not a default.** The loyalty plan leaves `customer_id` unset on issue for this reason (`:102`). A donation therefore does not require the recipient to have an account — the token in the link is the claim, and an account is created or linked at acceptance.
- **Stored value, so it surfaces under Loyalty.** `6.0-6.1-loyalty-gift-cards-store-credits.md:127`.
- **Codes never leak.** They do not appear in events, logs, serializers, share images or a buyer's own order confirmation; only the recipient's delivery carries the code (`:132`).
- **A subsidised or campaign card is a grant, not a purchase.** `*Welfare*` cards arrive without payment, so they need their own row recording who granted them, from which campaign, when they arrived and when they were claimed — otherwise the card's origin is unknowable and the liability is unaccounted for.

## Design Details

### Extending phase 2

The official phase 2 substrate is used unchanged: the product flag, denominations as variants, the recipient table, and issuance on `order.paid`. One boundary has to be drawn first, because phase 2 describes only one of the two channels this client has.

**Phase 2 describes the cart channel; the mini program uses the scenario channel.** A gift card bought on the storefront enters a cart as a product, carries recipient fields on its line item, and is issued by `Spree::GiftCards::IssueFromOrder` on `order.paid`. A gift card bought in the mini program is a **scenario order**: `gift-card/addOrder` sends `siteId`, `userId` and `orderGoodsList` — no cart, no line items, no address and **no recipient** (`miniprogram/pagesE/gift-card/details/details.js:355-395`) — and settles through `gift-card/payByWxMp`, with the card issued on settlement like every other scenario kind (`6.1-scenario-purchases.md`). The recipient is chosen later, by donating the card (below), and the blessing message travels with the donation rather than with the purchase. So the recipient table is the storefront's substrate and this plan's donation table is the mini program's, and each channel's issue step stays with its own channel. `IssueFromOrder` needs no discriminator and no guard: a scenario order never reaches `order.paid`, and the membership that shares the same product flag is bought through a scenario order too.

This plan adds:

- **Cover artwork** (`getCoverTypeSet`): a store-scoped catalogue of card designs, referenced by the recipient row so a card remembers what it looked like. It is presentation data, so it is a small store-owned table or a custom field, not a column on the card.
- **Activation** (`giftCardActive`): a card issued in a dormant state that the purchaser or recipient activates. The design half of Open Question 3 is now settled by the mini program, which ships the step for gift cards as well as for membership cards: the client calls `giftCardActive` with the card's own **code** when the card's status is `not_active`, and its detail sheet knows four further states — `used` (already activated), `expired`, `card_refund` and `frozen` (`miniprogram/pagesE/components/gift-card/detail-pop/detail-pop.js:60-77`, `miniprogram/pagesE/static/api/giftCardApi.js:28-30`). So a dormant status and an activation workflow are required, not optional. Two consequences the earlier draft did not carry: the client's five states do not map onto `Spree::GiftCard`'s four (`active`/`partially_redeemed`/`redeemed`/`canceled`, `gift_card.rb:21`) — `not_active` is missing, `frozen` and `card_refund` have no equivalent — and activation is addressed by the code, which is the bearer instrument, so the endpoint has to accept it rather than an id. Whether activation is a *regulatory* requirement is still unanswerable here; it no longer blocks, because the model below is the same either way.

### The ledger

An append-only table beside the card. Actions: `issue`, `redeem`, `refund`, `transfer_in`, `transfer_out`, `expire`. Each row carries the card, the action, a signed amount, the order or donation it came from, an actor (customer, admin or system), a memo and metadata. The card's `amount_used` and `amount_authorized` remain authoritative for the balance; the ledger explains how it got there and is what the balance-details page renders.

The open question is whether this belongs in core next to `StoreCreditEvent` or in the extension. Core is the consistent answer if gift-card history is a general need; the extension is the safe answer if it is not, and unlike the coupon holding, a ledger here decorates nothing — it only reads what the card already records.

### Donation and transfer

`spree_gift_card_donations`: `store_id`, `gift_card_id`, `from_customer_id`, `to_customer_id`, `to_phone`, `status` (`pending`/`accepted`/`canceled`/`expired`), `token` from `has_secure_token`, `message`, `expires_at`. The token is opaque and addressable without a session, so the mini program's `receiveGiftCardDonationUrl` works before sign-in, matching the shape already used for company invitations and the rule stated in `6.1-marketplace-message-relay.md`.

A donation does not move money until it is accepted. While pending, the card is held against being spent by the donor — the card already supports holds for this reason — and cancelling releases it. Accepting moves ownership and writes two ledger rows, `transfer_out` and `transfer_in`.

**A membership card is a different instrument and does not use this table.** `6.1-membership-and-loyalty-points.md` inherits none of the above: it owns its own dormancy, claim window and void against `spree_membership_cards` and `spree_membership_card_transfers`, and it states plainly that a membership is never stored as a `Spree::GiftCard`, so `gift_card_id` could not point at one even if it were wanted. The two share a **situation** — a card is bought dormant and activated later — and no mechanism. In particular a membership has no balance for a hold to sit against, so its pendency is a status rather than an `amount_authorized`, and the "release the hold, the balance never changed" rule below has no analogue there. Keeping the tables apart also removes the second subject this plan's transfer table would otherwise have had to accommodate: the fork described in `6.1-distribution-and-commissions.md` Open Question 1 is the distributor's alone.

### Welfare grants

`spree_gift_card_welfare_grants`: `store_id`, `gift_card_id`, `customer_id`, `granted_by_id`, `source` (`campaign`/`subsidy`/`promotion`), `arrived_at`, `claimed_at`. The card is issued immediately, as the store does owe it; the grant row records that it was not bought. A welfare card is not taxable on issue for the same reason a bought card is not (`6.0-6.1-loyalty-gift-cards-store-credits.md:92`): the tax is due on what the card later buys.

Purchases run through `6.1-scenario-purchases.md` with `kind: 'gift_card'`, so the twelve per-scenario payment endpoints collapse to one create plus a channel.

## Constraints on Current Work

- **Do not add a second prepaid-instrument flag or a second gift-card model.** `spree_products.gift_card` is the flag.
- **Do not let `Spree::GiftCards::IssueFromOrder` grow a discriminator.** It runs on `order.paid`, which only a cart order reaches. A scenario purchase creates no `Spree::Order`, so a mini program gift card and a membership — which share the `spree_products.gift_card` flag — never arrive there. Anything keying on the flag must still ask what kind of product it is before acting, but the issuer is not that place.
- **Do not put recipient or donation fields on `spree_line_items`.** Both have their own tables, for the reasons the loyalty plan gives at `:26`.
- **Do not let a card code into an event, a log, a serializer or an image.** Only the recipient's delivery carries it.
- **Do not model a bought card's sale as taxable on issue.** The tax follows what the card buys.
- **Do not permit paying for a gift card with a gift card or store credit.** The loyalty plan sets this at `:95` to stop one balance being laundered into another.
- **Do not release a pending donation's hold by editing the card's balance.** Release the hold; the balance never changed.
- **Do not stretch this plan to cover a membership card.** It is not a `Spree::GiftCard`, so `gift_card_id` cannot point at one and `spree_gift_card_donations` holds prepaid instruments only. `6.1-membership-and-loyalty-points.md` owns the membership card's two tables and its four transitions, and neither plan gates the other.

## Open Questions

1. **Does the ledger belong in core beside `StoreCreditEvent`, or in the extension?** Core is consistent and reusable; the extension is smaller and does not ask upstream to accept a concept it has not requested. Unlike the coupon holding, either is mechanically possible here. Unblocked by a ruling.
2. **What does "several gift cards on one order" mean now?** The loyalty plan deliberately keeps one card per order in 6.1 and defers the rest (`:22,138`). The mini program sells bundles and card products, which is several. Until this is answered, neither the purchase flow nor the coupon bundle in `6.1-coupon-wallet.md` can be built, because both are instances of it.
3. **Is an activation step required for prepaid instruments in this market?** Half answered, and the half that was missing is the one that blocked design. The **product** question is settled: the mini program ships a dormant-then-activate step for gift cards, not only for membership cards — `giftCardActive` is called with the card's own code when its status is `not_active`, and the same sheet knows `used`, `expired`, `card_refund` and `frozen` (`miniprogram/pagesE/components/gift-card/detail-pop/detail-pop.js:60-77`). So the status model has to carry a dormant state, and it has to reconcile the client's five states with `Spree::GiftCard`'s four. Whether it is a *regulatory* requirement is still not determinable from this repository, and the operator's compliance side should confirm it — but nothing waits on the answer now, because the model is the same either way. The membership plan reaches the same product conclusion from its own client evidence and **independently**: its card is dormant for the same reason, but it is not a gift card and does not need this ruling.
4. **Are welfare cards a marketing expense or a liability with a campaign attached?** The ledger treats them as value owed from the moment they are issued. If the operator instead recognises them only when claimed, the grant row needs a second state and the balance reader has to exclude unclaimed cards.
