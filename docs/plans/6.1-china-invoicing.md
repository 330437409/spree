## Summary

A Chinese 发票 is issued by the tax system, not by the shop. The platform never numbers it, never renders it, and never decides what it says — it collects a **request** from the customer, passes it to the issuing service, and hands back whatever images and files come out. The client models exactly that and nothing more: the form ends in "发票申请提交成功", the states are `0 开票中` and `1 已开票` (`pagesB/me/invoice/history/history.js:15-21`), and the artefacts are comma-separated URL lists — `invoiceFilePdf`, `invoiceImgUrl` (`pagesB/me/invoice/details/details.js:25-53`) — with a re-send mail that says it carries "PDF 文件、OFD 文件、XML 文件" (`pagesB/me/invoice/electronic/electronic.wxml:10,22`).

That is why the OSS rule this plan collides with is narrower than it first looks. `6.1-b2b-order-documents.md:90-91` says "**No invoice vocabulary in OSS** — no model, number, or document may be called an invoice", and the architecture plan adds "do not name a core model `Invoice`" (`:135`) while allowing a deliberate divergence to be recorded in a decisions log rather than worked around. This fork diverges from it, deliberately and in those words, for a gem that never leaves the fork: the thing genuinely is an invoice, calling it a "payment instruction" would be a lie in the other direction, and the concerns the rule protects — legal numbering, a PDF stack, country-specific issuance in core — are all absent here.

Two client facts shape the whole design. **The invoiceable base is not the order total**: it is `payMoneyNotVirtual`, the amount excluding virtual goods, which is why the form prints a warning when the order holds a membership card (`pagesF/invoice/invoice.js:81`) and why the settlement popup refuses to open for a gift-card payment (`pagesF/static/behaviors/settlementBehavior.js:146-166`). And **a site is a seller** (`fork-decisions.md` (2026-09-17, “A mini-program "site" is a seller”)), so the merge scope is one seller's orders and never the operator's whole order list.

## Key Decisions (do not deviate without discussion)

- **The request is our record; the document is an attachment.** `InvoiceRequest` holds what the customer asked for, who it may be issued by, which orders it covers and what it was worth; the issued artefacts are private-storage attachments plus the tax system's own identifier when it provides one. **We never mint an invoice number** — `6.0-document-numbers.md:117` is explicit that Spree's numbering "must never be marketed as legal invoice numbering", and a Chinese 发票号码 comes from 金税, so the gem has no numbering obligation at all.
- **The 抬头 is its own table, and `Spree::TaxIdentifier` keeps only the format verdict.** A title carries a name, a tax number, a registered address, a phone, a bank and an account; `TaxIdentifier` stores one `kind` and one `value` per owner (`spree/core/app/models/spree/tax_identifier.rb:53,72`) and cannot hold that shape. The tax number's *format* is validated through the existing registry — a `cn_*` kind added beside `eu_vat`, with a validator class (`spree/core/lib/spree/core.rb:180-197`) — and **no column and no `metadata` is added to `spree_tax_identifiers`**, the rule `6.1-vies-vat-validation.md:222` already states.
- **A merge is within one seller.** The client scopes every call by `siteId` and switching sites clears the selection (`pagesB/me/invoice/order/order.wxml:11-20`, `order.js:102-114,141,168`); `ordersInvoiceMerge` carries the site (`pagesF/invoice/invoice.js:550`). The operator does not issue one document across sellers, and the API refuses rather than silently splitting.
- **`selectAll` is re-resolved on the server, and the eligibility predicate is ours.** The client sends `selectAll: true, orderIds: null` (`invoice.js:550`); the server decides what "all billable orders at this site" means, and owes the client both the predicate and the per-order `reason` string the 不可开票 tab renders (`order.wxml:25-50`).
- **补开 is a deferred first issuance, not a correction.** The client's whole vocabulary is `isReplenishInvoice` on a completed order that never had one (`pagesF/static/template/order-info.wxml:30-33`). **红冲 (credit-note reversal) and 换开 are out of scope**, and the plan says so rather than leaving a reader to assume they were forgotten — 专票 is the case that normally needs 红冲, and the client offers none.
- **The invoiceable base is computed once and frozen.** It is the sum of the covered orders' non-virtual paid amounts, split into the two content lines the client's own note describes — 商品销售金额 and 配送服务金额 — remembering that "部分站点发票不含配送费" (`pagesF/invoice/invoice.wxml:140`).
- **Per-seller capability is configuration, never a constant.** The forms a seller supports are CSV strings (`invoiceTypeStr`, `invoiceSpecialType`) plus a 专票 switch (`provideSpecialTicke`), all supplied per site; the client reads them and shows only what they allow (`pagesF/invoice/invoice.js:74-79`). The gem stores them on the seller and answers the preview with them.
- **The word "invoice" is used, and that divergence is recorded.** See the Summary; the entry goes in `fork-decisions.md` in the same change that creates the gem — and nothing is written into upstream's plan, which is theirs.
- **The document is not an `OrderDocument`.** That model is order-scoped, buyer-uploadable and file-oriented (`6.1-b2b-order-documents.md:44-60`); an invoice request spans several orders and is issued to the customer, so it owns its row and only *borrows* the private-storage attachment pattern.
- **The vocabulary is invoice vocabulary, and the divergence is recorded (ruled 2026-09-18).** The models are `Spree::InvoiceTitle`, `Spree::InvoiceRequest` and `Spree::InvoiceDocument`, all inside the gem; `fork-decisions.md` carries the divergence with its boundary — nothing in `spree/core` or `spree/api` gains the word, and the gem is never proposed upstream. The neutral alternative would keep the letter of the OSS rule at the cost of calling a 发票 something other than its name. **Accepted trade-off:** if upstream ever ships its own invoicing product, the names collide — and a fork-only gem is the cheapest place for a collision to land.
- **The seller issues, and one request holds one seller's orders (ruled 2026-09-18).** The client scopes everything by site, a merge cannot cross sites, and `6.0-multi-vendor-marketplace.md:177` makes the seller merchant of record. Platform issuance under `tax_remittance: platform` is deferred until a store configures it, and the deferral is named rather than implied.
- **The tax service integration is out of scope (ruled 2026-09-18).** The gem owns the title, the request, the frozen amounts, the states, the attachments and the re-send; the issuance itself is an operator action in the admin plus the event that follows it. **Accepted trade-off:** a deployment without that action cannot issue — which is honest, where the alternative would promise an integration against a vendor nobody has chosen yet.
- **The merge preview is this gem's own read (ruled 2026-09-18).** It answers a different question from the price preview — which *historical* orders are billable, at which sites, for how much — so it is a separate read and the price preview keeps the settlement page's three capability fields and nothing more.

## Design Details

### Models

```ruby
create_table :spree_invoice_titles do |t|
  t.references :store, index: true
  t.references :customer, index: true
  t.references :company, index: true              # when the title belongs to a company, per 6.0-b2b
  t.string   :kind, null: false                   # person / company
  t.string   :title, null: false
  t.string   :tax_number
  t.string   :registered_address
  t.string   :registered_phone
  t.string   :bank_name
  t.string   :bank_account
  t.datetime :deleted_at
  t.timestamps
end
```

```ruby
create_table :spree_invoice_requests do |t|
  t.references :store, index: true
  t.references :customer, index: true
  t.references :seller, index: true               # one seller per request; a site is a seller
  t.references :invoice_title, index: true
  t.string   :mode, null: false                   # order / merge / replenish
  t.string   :form, null: false                   # electronic / paper
  t.string   :option, null: false                 # ordinary / special   (普票 / 专票)
  t.string   :status, null: false                 # requested / issuing / issued / failed
  t.decimal  :goods_amount, null: false, default: 0
  t.decimal  :delivery_amount, null: false, default: 0
  t.string   :currency, null: false
  t.string   :title_snapshot                      # see below
  t.jsonb    :payload                             # the capability verdicts, per respond_to?(:jsonb)
  t.timestamps
end
```

- `spree_invoice_request_orders` joins a request to the orders it covers, so "one request, several orders" and "an order appears in at most one live request" are both expressible as indexes rather than as hopes.
- The **snapshot** is the rule the tax-identifier model already learned: an invoice must show what was true when it was requested (`spree/core/app/models/spree/tax_identifier.rb:9-11`), so the title's fields, the receiving phone/email or the paper address, and the capability verdicts that allowed the choice are copied onto the request and never re-read from the title. This is the same freeze `TaxIdentifier` applies at completion, with one difference stated plainly: `decisions.md` (upstream, 2026-08-05: Tax provider contract carries treatments, identities and dates, “refined after the #14056 community review”) notes the order snapshot has "no correction path", and this plan keeps that — a wrong title is a new request, not an edit.
- A paper request additionally stores the recipient name, gender, phone and the assembled address with its **POI**, because the client folds the pickup point into the address string (`pagesF/invoice/invoice.js:409-419`).
- The issued side is its own row per document, because one request can produce several (`pagesB/me/invoice/overview/overview.wxml:11-22`): the tax system's identifier when it gives one, the content label (发票内容) and amount, and `has_many_attached :documents` on the private storage service for the images and PDFs. Paper adds the express pair (`expressCompany`, `expressNo`) — it is a physical mailing, "15-30 个工作日, 运费到付" (`invoice.wxml:26`), not a fulfilment, and it stays a pair of fields rather than a shipment.

### Capability configuration

Two CSV preferences and a boolean on the **seller**: which forms are supported at all, which of those support 专票, and whether 专票 is offered (`provideSpecialTicke`). The client's own default on the site record is `invoiceType: "1,2"` (`miniprogram/static/state/siteState.js:35`), which is the shape to keep. **The record they ride is `6.1-seller-service-area-routing.md`'s** (ruled 2026-09-18) — this plan stores them on the seller and reads them, and writes no site endpoint of its own. The preview answers with them (`6.1-store-api-miniprogram-gaps.md:25`), and the create re-checks them — a request that arrives with 专票 for a seller that does not offer it is refused with a structured error rather than downgraded, the same rule the WeChat gateway applies to an unknown scene.

### The eligibility predicate

An order may be invoiced when it is complete, it is the customer's, it belongs to the request's seller, it has not been covered by another live request, and its invoiceable base is above zero. The client renders the predicate's *message* per order (`reason`, `order.js:28-34`), so the API answers a list of `{ order_id, billable?, reason }` and the create runs the same predicate rather than trusting the list the client sends.

### Amounts

`goods_amount` plus `delivery_amount` equals the invoiceable base, computed from the covered orders' paid amounts with virtual goods excluded — the SVIP membership card is the case the client names (`pagesF/invoice/invoice.js:81`), and a gift-card-paid order is refused outright (`settlementBehavior.js:146-166`). Whether the delivery line is included is per-seller configuration, matching the client's note that some sites exclude it. All amounts are store-currency and stored as decimals; nothing here converts.

### Lifecycle, states and delivery

`requested → issuing → issued`, with `failed` for an issuance the operator must see. The client's two states map onto the ends of that ladder, so `0 开票中` is the first two and `1 已开票` is the third. The issuance itself is the operator's integration with the tax service — an admin-side action plus an event the gem listens for, not a job this plan invents; the request row, the attachment and the state transition are what the gem owes.

Notification goes through `6.1-notifications.md` (`:164`), which owns outbound messages, so `sendInvoiceEmail` is a call into it rather than a mailer of its own.

## Migration Path

1. The gem, its two tables (plus the join), `SingleStoreResource`, `Metadata` where it earns its place, and the three-place wiring.
2. Titles: the collection, the member, the create/update, and the `cn_*` validator registration beside `eu_vat`.
3. The preview read — eligibility, sites, totals and the capability verdicts — which is what the merge page needs before anything can be requested.
4. The request create, both modes, with the snapshot and the amounts frozen.
5. The issued side: documents, the attachment on private storage, the express pair, and the state transitions with the admin action.
6. The re-send through `spree_notifications`, and the admin surface for the operator (list, detail, mark issued, attach documents).
7. The divergence, recorded in `fork-decisions.md`: the deliberate use of invoice vocabulary. **Upstream's plan is not annotated** — this fork does not edit documents it does not own, and the divergence is stated where it belongs, in our own log and this plan.
8. Regenerate types, Zod, OpenAPI and SDK tests.

## Constraints on Current Work

- **Do not mint an invoice number.** The order number is the join key; the tax system owns the invoice's own.
- **Do not name a core model `Invoice`.** The vocabulary lives in the gem, never in `spree/core` or `spree/api` — and this fork's divergence from the OSS rule is recorded rather than assumed.
- **Do not add a column or `metadata` to `spree_tax_identifiers`.** The format verdict is what that model owes; the title's six fields are ours.
- **Do not merge across sellers**, and do not silently split a cross-seller selection either — refuse it.
- **Do not trust the client's selection.** `selectAll` and any `order_ids` are re-resolved against the predicate inside the create.
- **Do not re-read the title at render time.** The snapshot is what the document was asked for with.
- **Do not build 红冲 or 换开**, and do not leave the omission unstated.
- **Do not add a second notification mechanism**; the re-send goes through `spree_notifications`.
- **Do not attach documents to `OrderDocument`.** It is order-scoped and buyer-uploadable; a request spans orders.
- **Do not put the capability flags in code.** They are per-seller configuration the preview answers with.

## Open Questions

The four questions this plan opened — the vocabulary and its divergence, who issues, whether the tax-service integration is in scope, and where the merge preview lives — were settled on 2026-09-18 and now sit in **Key Decisions** with their trade-offs.

One item is genuinely deferred: **the platform-issued shape under `tax_remittance: platform`.** One request holding one seller's orders is the shape the client can express, and the marketplace plan's switch can move the issuing duty to the platform, which would need either a second request shape or a request covering several sellers. What unblocks it: a store actually configured that way, which does not exist today.

## Amendment to the OSS rule this plan relies on

`6.1-b2b-order-documents.md:90-91` states "**No invoice vocabulary in OSS** — no model, number, or document may be called an invoice", and the architecture plan (`:135`) permits a deliberate divergence, recorded in a decisions log rather than worked around. This fork takes that path, and the divergence is bounded on four sides:

- The vocabulary lives **only** in `spree/extensions/china_invoicing/` — no core model, no API resource and no locale key in `spree/core` or `spree/api` uses the word.
- The gem **mints no invoice number**; `6.0-document-numbers.md:117` keeps Spree's numbering out of the legal-invoice business and the Chinese number comes from the tax system.
- There is **no PDF stack and no rendering** — the document arrives from the issuing service and is stored as an attachment.
- The gem is **never proposed upstream**, which is the same boundary every extension in this set carries (`fork-decisions.md` (2026-09-17, “Mini-program feature gems live in `spree/extensions/`”)).

The rule's own purpose — keeping country-specific issuance, numbering and rendering out of OSS core — is therefore intact; what changes is only the word this fork uses for a 发票.
